Economics and Administrative Studies, Minor in Public Policy 📊🏫

My name is Vicky Rodriguez Solis. I am an undergraduate student attending UC Riverside majoring in Economics and Administrative Studies and minoring in Public Policy. I began as a community college student at Pasadena City College and transferred to UC Riverside beginning Fall quarter 2026. Since I’ve been here at UC Riverside I’ve learned how to acclimate to the quarter system and have learned plenty about economics, further solidifying my understanding of my own major and building upon the foundation that the lower division courses at Pasadena City College have built for me. It goes without saying that lower division courses are less likely to be as vigorous as some upper division courses, especially those of which that differ systemically, as in the difference between court systems and semester system systems. Since I have begun taking courses here, I think I have gathered an idea of what my next two years are going to look like, and I have gained a lot of confidence in my approach to being a successful student here.

My very first internship was an internship I had over the summer of my junior year high school at my school district, Pasadena Unified School District. I interned there in the summer of 2023 for about three months. I was shadowing a diverse equity and inclusion director for Pasadena Unified School District. That same year I also interned at my local. Credit union. Formally known as Pasadena Service Federal Credit union, I intern therefore about a year from 2023 until 2024. We hosted financial literacy workshops for my high school peers and we also were. We were also working as assistants who could help students sign up for checking or savings accounts. From 2024 until now, I’ve been working part-time as a food service worker.

After my two years at UC Riverside, I have plans to pursue public administration in the form of financial budgeting for a school district. If graduate school is a valuable option for me I would like to pursue a masters in public administration, possibly urban planning. If not, economics or public policy are also of interest to me. I’ve always been very interested in social sciences. A dual enrollment sociology class caught my eye when I was in high school. I’ve always been fairly skilled at math and after getting a small introduction to economics in my dual enrollment statistics class that I took in high school I had come to a realization that economics is fairly similar to sociology, but more quantitative. Since I’ve been at UC Riverside, I’ve been able to learn more about economic concepts that are specialized into different topics. And fun fact I initially wanted to be a math teacher, but found it to be a non-viable option to me as I wouldn’t have much of a return on my investment, so as a result, I resorted to studying economics and specializing in public education, so that at the very least, I could help operate schools in someway. And I like photography!
